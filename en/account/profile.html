Profile & Password

Everything account-related lives on the profile page.

Update your details

  1. Log in and open your profile.
  2. Edit your email, display name and other basics.
  3. Changes take effect as soon as you save.

Change your password

  1. Find the password area on the profile page.
  2. Enter your current password and a new one, then submit.
  3. You are asked to log in again with the new password.

If you have forgotten the current password, use Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

When the login state goes wrong

  • After a long time away you may be told the session expired; log in again.
  • Reading position, bookmarks and notes are stored with the account and are still there after logging back in.
  • If the browser blocks cookies, the login state may not persist.
