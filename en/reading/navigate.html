Paging & Position

Two ways to move: scroll in continuous modes, turn pages in paged modes. At the edge of a chapter you carry on into the next one.

Turning pages

  • Paged modes: click the left or right half of the text area; the arrow keys, PageUp / PageDown and Space (Shift+Space goes back) also work.
  • Continuous modes: just scroll up and down.

Moving between chapters

  • Keep going past the end of a chapter to enter the next one; go back past the start to enter the previous one.
  • Boundary Nav can be Auto or Manual: with Auto, reaching a boundary and continuing moves you on; with Manual you have to ask for it.
  • When you go back into the previous chapter, Previous Landing decides whether you land at its start or its end.

Resuming where you left off

Opening a book returns to the place you last read. To always start at the first page, turn off Restore Position in the Reading Behavior section of reading settings.

Width and margins

  • Width on the quick controls bar changes the width of the text; Fit returns to the recommended proportion.
  • In paged modes you can also set the top and bottom margins separately.
