Offline Reading

Offline Reading exists so you can keep reading when the network is gone.

Turn it on in Settings. Chapters you read from then on are cached automatically; you can also download the whole book at once to cache every chapter.

Turn it on

  1. Open Settings in the reader toolbar (each section is described in Reading Settings Overview).
  2. Expand the Reading Behavior section.
  3. Set Offline Reading to On. It is off by default.

Once it is on, a Download Offline button appears in the bottom-right corner of the reader. Turn it off and the button disappears; cached pages are no longer served.

Download the whole book

  • Click Download Offline in the bottom-right corner to cache the whole book: every chapter, the contents, the cover and the book info.
  • A progress bar shows while it runs; on completion you briefly see Offline ✓, then the button returns.
  • Click it again whenever you want to refresh the cache.

You can read offline without downloading, but only chapters you have opened on this device are cached. One click on Download Offline fills in the rest of the book.

While offline

  • An Offline Mode badge appears in the bottom-right corner.
  • Books you have downloaded open as usual — you can move between chapters and jump through the contents.
  • For a book you have not downloaded, or a chapter that is not cached yet, a message says the chapter is not cached; come back when you are online.

What does not work offline

  • Features that need a network: AI Translation, AI Chat, selection translation and the like.
  • Offline you read the original text; translations have to be generated online.

Cache and cleanup

  • Downloaded content lives in the browser on this device; another device has to download it again.
  • The cache is not tied to an account: logging in, logging out, switching accounts, or a session expiring clears the downloaded books on this device, and they have to be downloaded again.
  • There is no Clear cache button in the app. Setting Offline Reading to off only stops the cache from being served — downloaded content stays in the browser. To remove it completely, use the browser's Clear site data.
