Contents, Categories & Tags

The contents tree on the left gives you three ways to narrow the library down to what you are looking for.

Three entries

EntryWhat it means
SourcesWhich library or folder a book comes from
CategoriesGrouped by topic, maintained by the site or by you
TagsFree-form keywords; a book can have several

Filtering

  1. Expand the contents tree and click a category or tag.
  2. The Shelf shows only books in that group; the top bar shows the current filter and the number of books.
  3. To clear the filter, click the × next to it in the top bar, or go back to All.

Searching within a filter

With a category or tag selected, the search box looks only inside that group.
