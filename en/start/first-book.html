Find Your First Book

The Shelf — the site home page — is your library. Public books and your own books all live here.

Three ways to find a book

  • Just browse — the Shelf shows covers in a grid by default; turn pages at the bottom.
  • Narrow by contents — the contents tree on the left offers Sources, Categories and Tags. Pick one to show only that group.
  • Search — type a title or author in the top bar.

Open a book

  1. Click a cover, or a title in the list view.
  2. The reader opens with the reading settings you used last time.
  3. Your position is remembered, so the next visit returns to the same place.

Where books come from

  • Public library — sample books provided by the site.
  • Your own books — only you can see them. Upload files, or connect a Client Directory or network storage through Sources.
