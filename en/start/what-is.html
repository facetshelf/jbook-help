What is FacetShelf

FacetShelf is a multilingual reading platform that runs in your browser. Open a web page and start reading — there is nothing to install.

What you can do

  • Read almost anything — EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF and TXT. Bring your own books by uploading them, reading a folder on this computer, or connecting network storage (WebDAV or S3).
  • Read across languages — translate a book into another language and choose how the two texts are laid out: interlinear, translation only, or dual-column bilingual.
  • Ask about the book — AI Chat answers from the book itself, and citations jump back to the passage they came from, so you can check them.

What you need

  • A modern browser. A desktop browser gives you the full layouts, such as dual-column bilingual.
  • No account needed to browse and read. Sign in when you want more.
  • A small token allowance is included for trying AI Translation and AI Chat. You can also plug in your own model API key (BYOK).
