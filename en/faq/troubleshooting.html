Troubleshooting

A book will not open

  1. Refresh the page and try once more.
  2. Check that the book is EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 or TXT.
  3. If it was just uploaded, wait for the upload to finish before opening it.
  4. Large files take longer the first time they are opened.

No translation appears

  1. Check that a translation mode other than Off is selected in the AI Translation section.
  2. Check that the target language differs from the book's language.
  3. Translate while reading only translates where you are reading; go back to a translated part to see it.
  4. Check whether a quota warning is shown.

The AI does not respond

  1. Check whether it says the quota is used up.
  2. Wait a moment and try again, or ask a shorter question.
  3. If nothing happens for a long time, refresh the page and ask again.

I keep getting logged out

  1. Just log in again; your reading data is not lost.
  2. If it keeps happening, check whether the browser blocks cookies or is in private mode.
  3. Signing the same account in from several places in quick succession can produce a session-expired message.

A page still shows old content

Press Ctrl + F5 to force a refresh that bypasses the browser cache.
