FAQ

General

Do I need an account?

No. The site works without one, and your reading position, bookmarks and notes are kept as you go.

Where is my data stored?

FacetShelf is an online service. Library entries, reading positions, bookmarks, notes and AI conversations are stored on the site's servers, not locally. Books you upload privately are visible only to you.

Which browsers are supported?

A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Layouts such as dual-column bilingual look best on a desktop browser.

Do I need my own AI API key?

A small token allowance is included for trying AI Translation and AI Chat; you can also plug in your own model API key (BYOK).

Books and formats

Which formats are supported?

EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF and TXT.

Can I read DRM-protected books?

No. Books with DRM cannot be parsed.

Why does PDF not look ideal?

PDF is a fixed layout, which is hard to re-parse. For now a PDF is shown in its original layout; streaming reflow for PDF is planned.

Are there upload limits?

Yes. The size of a single file and the number of files in one batch depend on your plan; you are warned before uploading.

AI features

How long does translation take?

It depends on the range: translate while reading is nearly immediate, a chapter takes a little while, a whole book takes longest. You can keep reading while it runs — translated parts appear first.

Why does a citation have an outline but no highlight?

It means the AI used the meaning of that paragraph — summarizing or paraphrasing it — so no sentence from the original is used word for word. See Ask About This Book.

What if my AI quota runs out?

New AI requests report that the monthly quota is used up. Reading, bookmarks and notes are unaffected, and translated content already generated stays readable. See Quota & Usage.
